About

I’m an Applied Mathematics student at the University of Guilan with a strong focus on computer science, systems engineering, and software infrastructure.

My work centers around Linux systems, networking, and self‑hosted infrastructure. I design and operate small‑scale environments using tools such as Proxmox, Docker, and MikroTik networking while building backend services and automation around them.

Alongside systems engineering, I explore machine learning and computer vision projects, combining mathematical foundations with practical software development.

This portfolio documents the projects and experiments I build while learning about infrastructure, backend systems, and applied AI.
